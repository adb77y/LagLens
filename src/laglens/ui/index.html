A simple recording routine

  1. Open your game and select its rendering process.
  2. Choose your intended FPS target and record a repeatable scene.
  3. Press Ctrl+Alt+F8 when the hitch happens.
  4. Wait 5 seconds. The marked moment is saved automatically.
  5. Change one setting, repeat the route, then compare the two captures.

What the numbers mean

Average FPS
Frames divided by total frame time. A high average can hide brief hitches.
1% low FPS
1,000 divided by the average of the slowest 1% of frame times.
P99 frame time
99% of recorded frame times fall at or below this value.
Stutter event
Frames exceeding both 2× your target frame budget and 2.5× the run median. Nearby spikes merge into one event.

Evidence has limits

LagLens measures presented frames through PresentMon. System CPU and RAM readings are sampled separately. GPU busy time is shown only when provided by the capture source.

Network probing is optional and targets 1.1.1.1, not the game server. Correlation is not proof of cause. Shader compilation, paging and temperatures are not directly measured in this version.

Your data stays local

No account, analytics or background uploads. Captures are stored in your local LagLens folder.

Exports include the game name and performance data. Review them before sharing. Sample sessions are synthetic and always labelled.

Live capture uses Windows ETW. Some PCs require running LagLens as administrator. It does not change game files or inject into the game.
